About Swarnam4U

A simple rate board for buyers, sellers and jewellers in Kerala.

Loading rates…

Swarnam4U shows the Kerala board rate for gold, silver and platinum, so you can check the day’s price before you step into a shop. It is built for reference: a quick, clear look at the rate, its recent movement, and a calculator for estimating metal value.

Purpose

Make the daily Kerala board rate easy to read on any phone or computer, with the unit, currency and time of the last update always shown.

Supported metals

  • Gold: 24K (999), 22K (916), 18K (750), 14K (585)
  • Silver: 999 and 925
  • Platinum: 950

The calculator also offers other common fineness values, worked out from the reference board rate.

Rate information

The All Kerala Gold & Silver Merchants Association (AKGSMA) sets the daily board rate. This site reads that rate and refreshes it automatically on a schedule. Rates are in Indian rupees per gram and exclude GST, making charges and hallmarking.

If an update fails

The last verified rate stays on screen with its own timestamp, and a notice appears if it is old. When no verified rate exists, the site says so instead of showing a placeholder number.
